Why collect diapers?

  • Reduces landfill volume and leachate contamination
  • Cuts methane emissions from decomposing organics
  • Separates sanitary waste to protect public health

What we do: Pyrolysis ♻️

We apply pyrolysis—heating waste in the absence of oxygen—to convert diapers into useful outputs like oil, gas, and char. This lowers volume, recovers energy, and keeps neighborhoods cleaner.
